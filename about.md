# About Me

I'm Miguel Emilio Ruiz, a Software Engineer and Computer Engineer based in Madrid, Spain.

I have a background in Computer Engineering and professional experience developing software across different domains, including cloud computing, network security, education, media platforms, and public-sector systems.

Throughout my career, I've worked on a variety of software products, from backend services and distributed infrastructure to full-stack web applications. I've had the opportunity to contribute to both commercial products and open-source software, working with different teams and technology stacks.

## Engineering Background

My experience has primarily focused on backend and full-stack development, with a particular interest in the systems and infrastructure behind software products.

I've worked extensively with Ruby and Ruby on Rails, as well as Erlang, Elixir, JavaScript, and React.js. I've also used technologies such as Java, Python, Sinatra, Node.js, and Docker in different professional contexts.

Working across these technologies and domains has given me experience with different programming paradigms and engineering approaches, from object-oriented application development to functional programming and distributed systems.

## Areas of Interest

Some of the areas I've worked in and particularly enjoy exploring include:

- **Backend Development:** Designing and implementing application logic, APIs, and services.
- **Distributed Systems:** Understanding how independent components interact and building reliable software infrastructure.
- **Cloud Computing:** Working with cloud platforms, infrastructure provisioning, and automation.
- **Software Architecture:** Designing maintainable systems and evolving existing applications.
- **Open-Source Software:** Contributing to collaborative projects and working with publicly available codebases.

## How I Approach Software Development

I believe good software development starts with understanding the problem. Choosing the right technology is important, but understanding the requirements, the context, and the needs of the people using the software is just as important.

I value readable and maintainable code, automated testing, and collaboration through code reviews and knowledge sharing. I also enjoy learning new technologies and exploring different approaches to solving engineering problems.
